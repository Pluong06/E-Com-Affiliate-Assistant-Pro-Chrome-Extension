Get started in 3 steps

Takes less than a minute.

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to E-Com & Affiliate Assistant. Its blue shopping-bag icon stays one click away.

Save your affiliate IDs

Click the extension icon → Affiliate tab, fill in what you have, then Save:

  • Amazon — your Associates tag, e.g. yourtag-20
  • eBay — your Partner Network Campaign ID
  • Shopee — your affiliate ID (numbers only, e.g. 17334340417)
  • AliExpress / Etsy — your deeplink template containing {url}

Open any product page

On Amazon, Shopee, AliExpress, eBay or Etsy, a floating bar appears in the bottom-right corner:

  • 🔗 Copy Aff-Link — copies the clean product link with your ID
  • 🖼 Images — inspect the resolution & size of every photo and video
  • ⬇ ZIP and ＋ Compare — Pro features
💡 Tip: already had product tabs open before installing? Refresh them (F5) so the floating bar appears.

FAQ

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. The only external call is to Lemon Squeezy to check your license key. Read the privacy policy.

Do I need an affiliate account?

Only for the affiliate link feature — paste your own Amazon tag, eBay campaign ID, Shopee ID or deeplink template. All other features work without one.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open your Lemon Squeezy orders and cancel anytime. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

How many computers can I use?
